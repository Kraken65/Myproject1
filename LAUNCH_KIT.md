## ⚠️ Before you promote to strangers

Do NOT run paid campaigns or invite strangers until:
1. The bot runs **24/7 on a server** (see DEPLOY.md) — a dead bot kills trust instantly.
2. You've done **security hardening** — it's custodial; you hold their keys and their money.
3. You can **support users** — answer "why did my trade fail?" and handle fund issues.

Promote to a few friends first. Fix everything they hit. Then scale.
